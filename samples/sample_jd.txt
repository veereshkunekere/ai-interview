Backend Engineer (Python/Django) — CloudScale

About the role
We are looking for a Backend Engineer to design, build, and scale APIs that power our
SaaS platform. You will own services end-to-end: design, implementation, testing,
deployment, and monitoring.

Responsibilities
- Design and develop scalable REST APIs in Python (Django or FastAPI).
- Optimize PostgreSQL schemas and queries for high-throughput workloads.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and containerize services with Docker/Kubernetes.
- Integrate Redis-based caching and background job processing (Celery).
- Participate in on-call rotations and improve observability (metrics, logging, tracing).
- Collaborate with frontend and product teams to deliver features.

Requirements
- 3+ years of backend development experience with Python and Django.
- Strong SQL skills and experience with PostgreSQL in production.
- Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes in a cloud environment (AWS preferred).
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions or similar).
- Solid understanding of REST API design, authentication (OAuth2/JWT), and testing (pytest).
- Nice to have: Celery, Kafka, Terraform, Grafana.

Benefits
- Remote-friendly, flexible hours, learning budget, health insurance.
